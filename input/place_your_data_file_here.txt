This folder serves as path to store your data file.

Please be aware that your file must be one .xlsx file with one sheet.
In this sheet paste the historical closing prices for your two stocks and your market index, as well as the date of these prices.
Name the stock columns with your stock names, tickers, etc. whatever you want really :)
The market index column name must end with _INDEX (e.g. NASDAQ_INDEX).
It should look something like this:
Date | Stock name | Stock name | Stock Index_INDEX
